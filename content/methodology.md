The PPBS program comprises 12 datasets collected across three US election cycles (2016, 2018, 2020). Each wave includes nationally representative cross-sections and recontact panels, enabling both cross-sectional and longitudinal analyses. All surveys were fielded by professional survey companies using quota-based sampling to achieve nationally representative samples of the US adult population.

## Surveys by Sample Type
